How it works

  1. Tap "Enable orientation" and allow sensor access.
  2. Point your phone toward the direction you want to face.
  3. Tap "Set target to current heading" to lock the bearing.
  4. Move away from the target. Sound plays when the error reaches 4° or more.

If the phone is too flat or the browser denies sensor access, try again in a different orientation or use a supported browser.
